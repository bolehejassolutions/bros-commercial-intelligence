# BROS Commercial Intelligence — Product Brief

## Outcome

Provide BROS with an internal system for turning commercial evidence into structured intelligence, decisions, actions, outcomes, and reusable learning.

## Primary beneficiary

BROS internal operators responsible for product, sales, commercialization, and strategic decisions.

## Observable success

An operator can:

1. capture or ingest relevant evidence;
2. identify a meaningful signal;
3. open a bounded commercial case;
4. record diagnosis and competing explanations;
5. make and document a decision;
6. assign or execute an action;
7. record the outcome;
8. preserve the resulting learning for future decisions.

## Constraints

- Internal-first.
- Small initial surface area.
- Traceable evidence.
- Clear separation of fact, inference, decision, and outcome.
- No premature expansion into a general-purpose BI/CRM platform.
- No dependency on BROS SELL™ being redesigned.

## Initial milestone

Deliver a usable internal intelligence workspace that supports the complete evidence-to-learning loop for a small number of real BROS commercial cases.

## Non-goals

Do not initially build:

- customer accounts;
- public registration;
- community/membership functionality;
- full CRM;
- broad enterprise BI;
- predictive scoring without validated data;
- autonomous commercial decisions.

## Operating doctrine

BCI should improve decision clarity rather than create more dashboards.

A useful intelligence artifact must answer:

- What do we know?
- What changed?
- Why might it matter?
- What remains uncertain?
- What decision is required?
- What action follows?
- What happened afterward?
- What did we learn?
