# BROS Commercial Intelligence — Agent Context

## Product boundary

BCI is an independent internal application. Do not merge its architecture into BROS SELL™.

## Product objective

Turn authorised commercial evidence into traceable signals, cases, diagnoses, decisions, actions, outcomes and learning.

## Engineering principles

- Keep the MVP narrow.
- Preserve provenance.
- Separate observed facts from inference and decisions.
- Prefer explicit workflows over dashboard volume.
- Avoid speculative features and premature integrations.
- Do not introduce customer-facing access unless explicitly required.
- Do not add AI-generated conclusions without traceable evidence and human review.

## Current milestone

Application shell + domain model. Next work should make the end-to-end case workflow persistent and usable.

## Validation

Every meaningful source change should be build-checked before handoff where execution infrastructure permits.
